s-SNOM Spectrum Calculator

2026 · Software and research

An interactive Python application for calculating near-field amplitude and phase spectra of bulk and layered samples, and comparing calculations with experimental data.

The companion study extends the analytical spheroid model to multilayer samples and validates it against finite-element simulations and measurements of polymer films.

Publication: K. V. Voronin, E. Vicentini, L. Mester, R. Hillenbrand and A. Y. Nikitin, Quantitative Analytical Spheroid Model for Scattering-Type Scanning Near-Field Optical Spectroscopy of Layered Samples, arXiv preprint (2026).
